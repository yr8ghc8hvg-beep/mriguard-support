MRI Guard

Magnetic-field awareness for people who work around MRI

iPhoneApple Watch10 languages

An MRI scanner's magnet never switches off. Get too close with your iPhone or Apple Watch and the strong field can wipe or damage it — or pull it from your hand in an instant. It's an easy, expensive mistake.

MRI Guard adds a simple layer of awareness. It reads the magnetic field around your device and sounds a loud, unmistakable alarm as the field climbs toward the danger zone — giving you a moment to stop, check, and step back.

Made by an MRI physicist

MRI Guard is built by Dr. David Grodzki — MR physicist with a PhD in the field, holder of numerous MR patents, and multi-award-winning MR innovator, including the German Future Award for Technology and Innovation (Deutscher Zukunftspreis), presented by the German Federal President. It's built by someone who works with these magnetic fields first-hand and is deliberately careful about what the app claims: a smart awareness aid, never a replacement for approved MR safety equipment.

What it does

  • Live magnetic-field reading with a clear colour scale (green → red)
  • Adjustable alarm threshold — default at the 0.5 mT / 5-gauss controlled-area line
  • Loud alarm and vibration when the field crosses your threshold
  • Keeps monitoring in the background, even with the screen locked
  • Optional Smart Alarm to reduce false alarms from a magnet brought to a resting device
  • Units in microtesla (µT), millitesla (mT) or gauss (G)
  • Arrival reminder or automatic start at your saved workplace (iPhone), plus Shortcuts and Siri
  • Pauses itself while the device is charging — induction chargers create strong fields of their own — and resumes the moment you unplug
  • Warns you clearly when iOS Low Power Mode is on, which can delay background monitoring

iPhone & Apple Watch

The iPhone is the heart of MRI Guard: it can measure continuously in the background and start on its own when you arrive at work. This is where the app is at its best.

The Apple Watch app is genuinely useful on the wrist and is built to be as capable as watchOS allows. Start it with a tap on the watch (a watch-face complication makes it one tap), with a tap on your iPhone — or let it start along automatically whenever you start monitoring on the iPhone. Alarms from the iPhone are mirrored to your wrist as well. Because Apple doesn't let watch apps monitor location in the background, the watch itself can't react to arriving at work — that remains the iPhone's job. We've made the most of what the platform permits.

Arriving at work — good to know

Save your workplace and choose what happens when you get there: an arrival reminder (a notification — one tap opens the app and starts monitoring; the dependable choice) or automatic start, which arms monitoring hands-free. Monitoring stops by itself when you leave. This uses Apple's geofencing; for reliable detection allow Always location access with Precise Location on, keep Wi-Fi enabled (iOS uses it to recognise places, even without a connection), and avoid Low Power Mode. iOS typically reports an arrival one to three minutes after you reach a saved place — and opening the app always starts monitoring right away. For large buildings you can increase a place's detection radius in the app.

Private by design

No account. No sign-in. Your saved locations stay on your device and are never uploaded. The app can share anonymous, aggregate usage statistics to help improve it — you can turn that off anytime in Settings. See our Privacy Policy.

Please read. MRI Guard is an awareness aid — not a certified safety device or medical instrument. The magnetic sensor in a phone or watch is uncalibrated and saturates at a few millitesla, so it cannot measure strong fields accurately and may not react in every situation. Never rely on it for safety-critical decisions. Always follow your site's MR safety rules and use approved measuring equipment. You are responsible for your own safety and your devices.
